I'm Trond Bordewich, a developer and architect living in Trondheim. These days I work as a consultant at [Kodeskalle](https://kodeskalle.no), together with Erling and John.

I've been doing this for a while. I started out with industrial automation and robots, moved on to in-house applications, and spent a good few years making apps for macOS and iOS. Since then I've wandered through Clojure, Go, TypeScript and Elm, and lately a lot of C# and .NET. I like picking up new languages, mostly because each one changes how I think about the others.

One thing hasn't changed: I learn best by sharing what I discover. That's why this blog exists. It's been quiet for a few years (ten, actually), and why is a story for a post of its own. But I'm back.

When I'm not programming I'm usually climbing, indoors or out on the rock. Now and then I go running, skiing or biking in the woods around Trondheim. I also love spending time with my wife and our two daughters.
