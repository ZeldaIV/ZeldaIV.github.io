My goodness, it's time to give this blog a fresh start!

10 years ago, I started this blog as a way to share my thoughts and experiences with the world. However, times have changed, and it's time for a fresh start.

First of all, the blog really needed to be migrated over from the old jekyll setup.

In this business 10 years is such a long time, that the praise I had for what was fancy back then (you can read about it [here](this-is-not-the-same-site-as-last-time.md)), is definitely not what the cool kids use today.
So I opted in for [hugo](https://gohugo.io) this time around, and I'm already loving it. It removed so much boilerplate and dependencies on other tools. And will definitely make my blog-life easier.

I have so much news to share since the last time I wrote a blog post. I'm excited to share my journey with you all.

A sneak peak on what's to come:
- Why was I away for such a long time?
- I built my own mailserver with postfix, dovecot, and rspamd.
- It inspired me to build a mail service that you can use for your domain, you check it out over at [Misralo](https://misralo.com).
- How does auth work when setting up your own mcp server?
